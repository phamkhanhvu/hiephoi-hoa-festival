Open letter

Dear members, businesses, organisations and individuals interested in the Festival Hoa Đà Lạt 2026,

The Festival Hoa Đà Lạt is not only a celebration of brilliant colours, but also an occasion to honour everyone who has helped build the value of the flower industry, from farmers, artisans and scientists to businesses, distributors and partners.

The Dalat Flower Association is carrying out the programmes of the 11th Festival, including exhibitions, trade, experiences and community connection.

Partnership may take the form of funding, fresh flowers, plants, products, equipment, services or suitable professional resources.

On behalf of the Dalat Flower Association WARMLY INVITING YOUR PARTNERSHIP! Chairman
