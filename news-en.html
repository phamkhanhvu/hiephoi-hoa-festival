The 11th Festival Hoa Đà Lạt – 2026 is preparing to open

A programme running from 10/12/2026 to 03/01/2027, with the opening ceremony planned for 19/12/2026.

The programme includes the 20th anniversary celebration of the Association, flower exhibition spaces, the "Dance of Colours" gala, a flower-decorated bicycle parade and flower float, the Vegetable & Flower Market, a scientific and technology seminar for the flower industry, and experience tours of flower-growing areas in Lâm Đồng.

The Festival is carried out under the direction of the Lâm Đồng Provincial People's Committee, in coordination with partners and members of the Association.

Festival Hoa Đà Lạt

Diverse persimmon products – autumn specialties of Đà Lạt welcome the Festival Hoa

18/10/2026 · Festival Hoa Đà Lạt Information Portal

Ahead of the 11th Festival Hoa Đà Lạt, persimmons are drawing attention as one of the autumn specialties. Wind-dried persimmons are made from about September over roughly three months, depending largely on the weather and seasonal labour.

Producers in Xuân Trường, Xuân Sơn and Xuân Hương are diversifying their products, from dried persimmons and persimmon wine to sweets, while opening their orchards and processing workshops to visitors. Many also sell online to reach customers outside the province.
